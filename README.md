# Çatalhöyük · Archaeological Field Journal & Stratigraphic Matrix
### Antique Rustic & Futuristic Cyber-Archaeological Edition 🏛️🌐

An experimental 3D WebGL archaeological information system and stratigraphic field journal mapping the 9,000-year-old Neolithic settlement of Çatalhöyük (7100–5700 BCE). Built with Three.js and custom spatial raycasting, this platform bridges high-resolution bioarchaeological research with public heritage storytelling through a specialized antique parchment and rust-toned aesthetic blended with futuristic spatial HUD elements.

> **⚠️️ Prototype Notice:** This project is a functional design prototype intended for data visualization and digital humanities exploration. Details within the prototype may not be 100% historically or archaeologically exact; the primary purpose is to digitize information and conceptualize spatial layout. All artifact geometries and placements are placeholders and can be replaced with actual high-fidelity 3D models or scanned assets as needed.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **Interactive 3D Stratigraphic Navigation:** Seamlessly toggle between an overarching excavation master plan featuring 7 distinct ancient structures (complete with timber roof access ladders and hatch openings) and granular interior visualizations spanning temporal horizons from 7100 BCE to 5700 BCE.
* **Dual Audience Modes:** Instantly switch between **Academic** view (featuring micromorphological data, carbon/soot pathology markers, isotope ratios, micro-XRF telemetry, and peer-reviewed citations) and **Public** view (focused on engaging domestic life, ritual symbolism, and daily Neolithic routines).
* **Detailed Artifact & Feature Inspection:** Inspect 3D-modeled archaeological relics in situ—including plastered ancestral crania, obsidian caches, aurochs horn installations, unbaked clay boiling spheres, and wall murals.
* **Micro-Stratigraphic Data Matrix & HUD:** Deep-dive modal inspection panes covering bioarchaeology, lithics, pyrotechnology, and architectural phasing, enhanced with real-time camera-projected UI labels, particle atmospheres, and custom rustic wireframe architectural shaders.
